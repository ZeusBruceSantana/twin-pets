# Twin Pets

A two-player virtual pet game for two sisters, played side by side at the
same time on one touch screen held sideways (an iPad or a Microsoft Surface).

- **Left side:** Leah and her snow leopard, **Livvie** (honey fur with black-and-brown spots)
- **Right side:** Avery and her puppy, **Avery Peach**

Each girl has her own big buttons along her edge of the screen. The pets
play together in the shared middle. There are no scores, no winners, and the
pets never get sick or sad. If nobody plays, they just wait happily.

The game is a few plain files (`index.html`, `styles.css` and the `js`
folder). It works without internet once it has been opened once, installs on
the home screen like an app, and needs no accounts and no outside servers.

Each room has its own soft background tune, written by the game itself. The
little ♥ at the bottom of the start screen opens a page that says who made it.

---

## How to play (for grown-ups)

**First time:** each girl taps a color on her half. They can't both pick the
same one. Then tap the big green ▶ in the middle. After that, the game opens
on the start screen: tap anywhere to play.

### The shared middle

The middle of the screen belongs to both girls. In every room it has:
- the **friendship jar** at the top. Every together moment adds a heart in both girls' colors.
- the **house map** button (a little house). Tap it to see the whole house.
- the **camera**, which takes a picture of the game for the memory book.
- the **peekaboo** button for a little brother: an animal peeks out and says hello. No reading needed.

### Moving around the house

Tap the **house map**. Each room on the map has both pets' faces on it:
Livvie on the left half, Avery Peach on the right. **To go somewhere, both
girls tap the same room within a few seconds**, each on her own half. When one
girl taps, the room glows and her sister's pet face blinks to invite her. Both
pets always go together.

A gold star on the map means something new is waiting in that room. A little
present means the jar is full and presents are waiting in the playroom.

### New things, a few at a time

The house has lots to do, but it doesn't all appear at once. Each time the
game is opened, **up to three new things introduce themselves**, one at a
time, a minute or so apart. Each one shows its picture and name in the middle
(tap the word to hear it), and its button pops in with a sparkle. If it's in
another room, that room gets a star on the map. Brand-new players learn the
basics first.

### The rooms

Each room shows **only its own buttons** along each girl's edge.

| Room | Buttons | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 🧸 **Playroom** | Play, Tricks, High five, Puppets, Tea party, Vet | Both tap **Play** close together and the pets chase each other. **Tricks** shows word cards (sit, spin, roll over, wave, dance): tap a card to hear the word and the pet tries it. After 3 tries it learns it. **Puppets**: each girl picks a prop (wand, microphone, top hat, cape), the curtains open, and her pet shows off its tricks, then both take a bow. **Tea party**: set the table, invite a stuffed friend (elephant, giraffe or octopus), pour pretend tea, and sip. **Vet**: listen to a silly heartbeat, tap the knee to make the leg kick, and give a sticker. The pets are never sick. |
| 🍲 **Kitchen** | Fridge, Cook, Picnic, Treat | **Fridge** opens a panel of foods with pictures and words (fruit, veggies, pancakes, pizza, ice cream, fish, bone...). Livvie's favorite is **fish** and Avery Peach's is **peaches**: they get a happy dance. **Cook**: pick a smoothie, pizza or birthday cake, then one girl adds an ingredient and her sister stirs, taking turns. **Picnic**: both pets sit at the table and eat together. **Treat** tosses a treat to the sister's pet. The tummy gets rounder as the pet eats and empties over about an hour. |
| 🛁 **Bathroom** | Bath, Teeth, Brush, Dryer, Spa | **Bath** fills a tub. While her pet is in the bath, a girl's buttons become bath toys: a **boat**, a **squirty fish**, and **fizzies** that change the water color. **Teeth** ends in a sparkly smile. **Dryer** makes the fur poof up big and funny, then settle. **Spa** pampers the **sister's** pet with paw polish and a bow (in the giver's color) and sparkle spray, for the rest of the day. Tap a pet's face in the **mirror** to make a funny face. |
| 🌳 **Backyard** | Bounce, Climb, Ball, Seesaw, Garden | Trampolines, the rainbow climb (never a race), and the ball and seesaw games. **Garden**: Plant seeds, Water them, and they grow over real time (about 20 minutes). **Pick** what's ready and it goes into the fridge. |
| 🛏️ **Bedroom** | Sleep, Book, Closet, Friends, Sleepover | **Sleep** both pets for the goodnight ending. **Book** is the memory book. **Closet** has everything each pet can wear; tap to put it on or take it off. It is the very next new thing to appear once either pet has been given something to wear, so nobody is left stuck in a scarf. **Sleepover**: the lights go down, the pets snuggle in a blanket fort, and each girl shines a flashlight to show shadow pictures (tap the word to hear it). |
| 🌳 **Treehouse** | Catch, Weather | **Catch**: both tap it; each pet holds a basket on her head, and the girls drag (or use the big arrows) to slide her under the falling fruit. A miss just bounces away. Both baskets fill a smoothie. **Weather**: mix sun, cloud, rain, snow and wind: sun + rain make a rainbow, sun + wind make kites, cloud + snow make a snowman, cloud + rain grow flowers. A little chart fills in as they find each one, and finding all four is a prize. |
| 🎸 **Garage** | Jam, Pick, Band, Dance | **Pick** an instrument (guitar, keys, drums, mic, tambourine or trumpet); the pet holds it. **Jam** plays a note, and every note is in the same happy scale, so it always sounds good (both jam at once and a chord rings out). **Dance** (both tap it): paw prints drift down to each pet; tap when one reaches her ring and she does a dance move (a miss is just a wiggle), and a big star needs both girls for a twin spin. **Band**: both tap it and the show begins on a little stage with curtains and spotlights: each girl plays ten notes over a soft backing beat, and her **animal friends are the cheering crowd** (they dance along, then cheer at the big finish). |
| 🏡 **Front yard** | Mail, Car, Hello | The picture behind it is a **drawing** (see [Room pictures](#room-pictures-the-front-yard-and-backyard)). **Mail** (or tap the mailbox) reads letters written in the grown-up corner. Every word can be tapped to hear it. **Car**: both girls tap the car, then choose together where to go. When a visitor comes, **Hello** greets them. |
| 🏫 **School** | Words, Art, Blocks, Puzzle | **Words**: the pets practice this week's school words on the chalkboard. Each girl taps to hear the word and her pet says it; when both have, a star and the next word. **Art**: one big canvas, half each. Finger-paint, tap **Done**, then both choose a room on the map to hang it in. **Blocks**: build a doghouse, a cozy den or a blanket fort, taking turns adding blocks in your own color. |

### Trips in the car

From the front yard, both girls tap **Car**, then both tap the same place:
- 🏖️ **the beach**: splash in the waves, build a sandcastle together, find shells
- 🌳 **the park**: a calm place with ducks on the pond (and Bubbles, below)
- 🍦 **the ice cream shop**: scoop flavors onto a cone (fish flavor is Livvie's favorite!), add sprinkles, lick

- 🛝 **the playground** (a new thing that appears on the town map): four things to do. **Super swing**: each girl taps **Pump** to push her own swing higher; when it is high, a big **Jump!** button appears and her pet lets go, flies into the pile of leaves ("Whee!") and goes back to her swing. Each jumps twice, then it ends happily. **Slide**: her pet climbs the ladder and whooshes down the twisty slide into the sandpit. **Spin**: a merry-go-round with the two pets' faces and animal friends; every push speeds it up, more when both girls push, and at top speed there is confetti and a cheer, then it eases down. **Dig**: each press digs up a treasure (bone, ball, star, heart, duck, shell); six treasures bring a golden star, and the first golden star each day earns a prize
- 🫧 **the park** also has **Bubbles**: each pet blows bubbles, the girls pop them (every pop is a rising note), and then a giant bubble with both pets inside needs both girls to tap it together
- 🐑 **the farm**: five farm animals live here (a hen, horse, cow, lamb and piggy: tap one to hear it), and four games, each with a goal and a finish. **Milk**: one girl holds up the **Bucket** while the other **Milks** (a pulse ring shows a steady beat); the bucket fills, and halfway they swap jobs. **Eggs**: eggs and sleepy chicks hide under eight hay piles, **Sniff** makes a pile with something under it hop, and when six eggs are in the basket a golden egg needs both girls to tap it. **Feed**: each animal shows a picture of what it likes; any food is fine and the right one makes it dance, and when all five have danced the whole farm cheers. **Tractor**: both pets ride; one girl steers up and down, the other **Honks** to move animals out of the road, and they swap every lap while the hay bales fill the wagon
- 📚 **the library**: four games. **Story**: each girl picks a picture card for a hero, a place and a thing (tap any picture to hear its word); her storybook picture builds up, and when both are done the pets act the stories out. **Shelve**: each girl has her own bookcase and taps the shelf that matches each book (a wrong shelf just wiggles, and the right one glows after two tries). **Hide**: one girl taps a book stack to hide the friend, the other finds it (the hider's **Giggle** is a clue), then they swap for four rounds. **Shh**: both pets tiptoe to the reading corner past the sleepy librarian cat; when she looks (the shh sign) they freeze for a moment, nothing is lost
- 🎡 **the fun fair**: a **claw machine** (one girl slides, one drops, and they swap jobs every round; it always wins in the end, and the first win each visit is a prize), **pop-ups** (critters peek out of holes and giggle when booped; a golden twin critter needs both girls) and a **ferris wheel** that both pets ride
- ⛷️ **the snowy hill**: sled, make a snow angel, toss a gentle snowball to your sister's pet
- 🎣 **the river**: fish (and let the fish go), sail a little boat, skip stones
- ⛺ **the woods**: build a tent together, light a campfire and toast marshmallows, look at the night sky and join the stars into constellations (a kite, heart, spoon, crown, fish, cat, boat or tree; each shows its name, then a new sky appears)
- 🖼️ **the art museum**: **Art** shows the same little picture painted seven ways (real, dots, bold, brushy, shapes, dreamy, splash: Realism, Pointillism, Pop Art, Impressionism, Cubism, Surrealism and Abstract). Each style has its word, its real name and a few words about it (tap to hear), and the pet joins in with an effect. See all seven for a prize. Also strike a statue pose and hang each pet's portrait. The puzzle asks "which one is made of dots?"
- 🍽️ **the fancy restaurant**: **Pip the penguin waiter** greets you and carries each dish on a tray (the silver lid lifts with a flourish). The menu has nine fancy dishes that are only served here (soup, salad, sushi, spaghetti, tacos, crepes, macarons, tart, sundae), and each pet puts on a napkin. **High tea**: the waiter wheels in a tiered stand of treats, each girl pours, nibbles a treat and sips with her pinky up, then they clink cups. Or just clink glasses.

The car opens a **town map**: a little illustrated map with the sea and beach, a river running down to it, woods, snowy mountains, farm fields and roads joining the ten places (places not yet found show as a ? cloud). The farm, library,
snowy hill, river, woods, museum and restaurant appear one at a time, like other new
things. Places with someone new to meet have a star.

Both tap **Home** to drive back.

### Animal friends

At some places (the farm, the park, the library) and when a visitor comes, both
girls tap **Hello** and the animal becomes a **friend**: Clover, Hoot, Woolly,
Daisy, Truffle, Lily, Puddle, Whiskers, Squeak, Ollie, Tilly, Dotty, Bramble, Rusty,
Bao, Pip, Frost, Biscuit the horse and Clucky the hen. Two more (Sparkle the unicorn and Ember the dragon) only come as
puzzle prizes. The **Friends** button in the bedroom
lets each girl pick **one friend at a time** to sit beside her pet (tap again to
put it back). Two girls can't have the same friend at once. Tap a friend to hear it.

### Little surprises

- **Visitors:** sometimes Clover the bunny or Hoot the owl knocks at the front
  door. Go to the front yard together and say **Hello**.
- **Real stuffed animals:** about once a play session, each pet asks for
  something from its real stuffed animal, like *"Give Livvie a real hug!"*
  Tap the heart when it's done.
- **Weather:** some days it rains (jump in the puddles, then it's bath time!).
  In winter it can snow, and the girls can build a snowman in the backyard.
- **Halloween:** all of October, the closet has cute costumes (pumpkin hat,
  witch hat, bat wings), and pumpkins sit by the front door.
- **Birthday:** on the pets' birthday (set it in the grown-up corner), there's
  a party with balloons and a cake. Both girls blow out the candles.

### Puzzles

Each girl solves **her own half** of a puzzle. There are seven kinds: *what comes next?*, *which one is different?*, *whose shadow is this?*, *how many?*, *find the pairs* (a wrong pair just turns back over), *copy the tune* (a wrong pad replays the tune), and a jigsaw where each girl has the pieces for her side of the picture.
The puzzles are all pictures, with no timer and no losing: a wrong try just wobbles, and
after two the right answer glows. They are at the school (**Puzzle**) and at the river,
woods, museum and restaurant.

When both halves are done, **both girls get a prize**. They alternate: two presents for the house (one from each box; both open them, then choose a room for each on the map), or a **gift for each pet** (something to wear, or a toy for the shelf) that flies to her pet. Every third puzzle also brings a special new animal friend.

### The jar, presents and decorating

**When the jar is full**, two wrapped presents arrive in the playroom. Each
girl taps **Give** to send hers to her **sister's pet**: something to wear,
or a toy for its shelf. Then there's a party, and then **the house gets a
present too**: wallpaper, a rug or furniture (a plant, a lamp, a beanbag, a
fish bowl). Both girls tap **Open**, then both choose a room on the map to put
it in. Puzzle prizes come the same way.

There are about sixty-six things to win: **wall paint colors**, **wallpapers** (dots, clouds,
rainbow, flowers, waves, moons and more), **rugs**, **furniture** (sofa, chair, bookshelf,
toy box, table, clock) and **little electronics you can tap**: a radio that plays a tune
and floats music notes, a TV that shows the animal friends, a music box with a dancer, a
disco ball, a night light, a robot. Each room holds a rug, a wall (paint or wallpaper) and
up to three pieces of furniture. More to win: a piano, a fish tank, a globe, a telescope, a record player, a boombox, a lava lamp, a doll house and a cactus (most do something when tapped), 8 more paint colors, 7 more wallpapers and 3 more rugs. Things for the pets: a party hat, wizard hat, headphones, bandana, rainbow band, shades, bow tie, medal, cape and fairy wings, and toys (kite, train, top, xylophone, duckie). The new things to wear also come as jar presents.

### Reading

**Tap any word to hear it read aloud**: names, room names, thought bubbles,
trick cards, letters, school words, "Thank you", and everything in the memory
book. **Thought bubbles** have a picture and one word.

**The memory book** gets one short sentence a day about what the pets did
together, plus the pictures taken with the camera that day (tap a picture to
see the next one).

## The hidden grown-up corner

**Press and hold the top-left corner of the screen for 3 seconds.** A ring
fills up, then the grown-up panel opens. Everything typed here stays on this
tablet.

- **Backup**: save everything to a backup file, or restore from one. See
  [Backups](#backups-keep-their-progress-safe) below. The tile shows the date
  of the last backup, and glows gently if it's been more than a month.
- **Pictures**: choose a photo or drawing for the front yard or the backyard.
  See [Room pictures](#room-pictures-the-front-yard-and-backyard) below.
- **Write a letter**: type a short letter. It goes in the mailbox in the front
  yard, and the front yard gets a star on the map.
- **School words**: type this week's words with commas between them
  (`cat, sun, play`). The pets practice them at pet school. Leave it empty for
  starter words.
- **Home Core**: connect the game to the family's Home Core app. In Home Core, a parent opens **More**, **Settings**,
  **Game link**, makes the link and copies it; paste it here and tap **Connect**, then choose which child goes with
  each pet (Home Core shows each child as her color and symbol, like "Lavender star": it never sends anyone's name).
  While the game is open it looks every few minutes: pet school practices each girl's **words of the week**, and her
  pet cheers once when **all of today's jobs are done** and once for each **reward a parent approves**. **Disconnect**
  forgets the link. If the link stops working (a parent made a new one), this tile says so.
- **Kindness hearts**: saw them being kind to each other? Add a heart (or a
  few) to their jar. They drop in when you close the panel.
- **Play timer**: Off, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. When time is up, the
  pets yawn, go to the bedroom and fall asleep. **They stay asleep until a
  grown-up wakes them here** (a **Wake the pets** button appears), even if the
  game is closed and opened again.
- **Pets' birthday**: tap a month and set the day.
- **Sounds** and **Music**: two separate switches (music is on, quietly, to
  start), and the **volume**
- **change colors** (each girl picks again)
- **show all new things now**, which is handy for trying everything at once
- go **full screen** (on the Surface, when not already full screen)
- **start over**, which erases everything. Tap it twice to confirm.

## Put the game online with GitHub Pages (free)

GitHub Pages turns this repository into a web address you can open on the
iPad or Surface. You only need to do this once.

1. First, merge the pull request so the game is on the **main** branch.
   (On the pull request page, click **Merge pull request**, then **Confirm merge**.)
2. On GitHub, open this repository and click **⚙️ Settings** (top of the page).
3. In the left-hand menu, click **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment** → **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Under **Branch**, choose **main** and **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
6. Wait a minute or two, then refresh the page. A box will say
   **"Your site is live at …"**. For this repository the address will be:

   **https://zeusbrucesantana.github.io/twin-pets/**

Whenever the files on the main branch change, the website updates by itself
within a few minutes. If the tablet still shows the old version, close the
game completely and open it again.

> The repository needs to stay **public** for free GitHub Pages. The game
> keeps nothing private online. The girls' progress, letters and school words
> stay on the tablet. When Home Core is connected, the game only reads from it
> (words, how many jobs are done, approved rewards; never a name), and the link
> is kept on the tablet too.

## Add it to the home screen (full screen, like an app)

### iPad

1. Open the game's address in **Safari**. (It has to be Safari.)
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open Twin Pets from the new home-screen icon. It opens full screen with
   no address bar.

Tips:
- To stop the screen from turning, hold the iPad sideways, open **Control
  Center** and turn on **Rotation Lock**.
- To keep the girls inside the game, use **Guided Access**: *Settings →
  Accessibility → Guided Access*. Then triple-click the top (or home) button
  while the game is open.
- **No sound?** Check that the iPad isn't on silent (in Control Center, the
  bell should not be crossed out) and that the volume is up.
- The home-screen app keeps **its own save**, separate from Safari. Always
  open it the same way, from the home-screen icon. If the girls have been
  playing in Safari, make a **backup** there first, then **restore** it in
  the home-screen app (see [Backups](#backups-keep-their-progress-safe)).
- After it has been opened once with internet, the game **works without
  internet** too.

### Microsoft Surface

1. Open the game's address in **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Click the **…** menu (top right) → **Apps** → **Install this site as an app**
   (it may say **Install Twin Pets**), then **Install**.
3. Open Twin Pets from the Start menu or taskbar. It works without internet
   after the first time.
4. Tapping the big ▶ to start makes it go full screen. You can also press
   **F11**, or use **Full screen** in the grown-up corner.

Tips:
- Take the keyboard off (or fold it back) so the Surface is in tablet mode.
- To keep it sideways: *Settings → System → Display → Rotation lock*.
- Swiping in from the very edge of the screen opens Windows menus. The game
  can't block that, so the buttons sit a little in from the edge.

## Saving

The game saves by itself on the device it is played on. It saves:
- the colors, the jar, and which new things have appeared
- the memory book and its pictures
- each pet's tummy, mud, fluffy fur, learned tricks, presents, clothes and spa look
- the garden, the fridge, paintings on the walls, decorations and room pictures
- letters, school words, the play timer and the pets' birthday

Updates to the game keep the save. If you clear the browser's website data,
the save is erased too, so make a backup now and then. The game never sends
anything anywhere.

If the tablet ever runs out of room for the game, the oldest camera pictures
are let go first, so everything else keeps saving.

## Room pictures: the front yard and backyard

The front yard and the backyard can show a **drawing or photo** instead of the
built-in scenery. The front yard comes with a drawing of a purple house (with a
rainbow, a sun and a little blue bird). The pets, the mailbox, the car, the
trampolines and the garden all sit on top of it.

**To change a picture, right on the tablet** (nothing to edit, nothing to
upload):
1. Open the grown-up corner (hold the top-left corner for 3 seconds) and tap
   **Pictures**.
2. Tap **Choose a photo** next to the front yard or the backyard.
3. On the iPad, pick a photo from the library, or choose **Take Photo** to
   photograph a drawing right then. On the Surface, pick a file.
4. Done. The new picture shows straight away, and the little round picture of
   the room on the house map changes too.

**Tips:** wide pictures (landscape) fit best, and the middle of the picture is
what stays visible. If a photo has a lot of table around the drawing, crop it
first in the Photos app. Tap **Use the original** to go back to the picture that
came with the game (or the built-in scenery).

Pictures you choose are saved **on the tablet only**, and **backups include
them**. Restoring a backup on a new tablet brings them back too.

*For the game's own pictures:* the front yard's drawing lives in
`art/rooms/frontyard.jpg`. Which picture each room starts with is set in
`ROOM_ART` in `js/settings.js`.

## Backups: keep their progress safe

A backup is one small file with **everything**: colors, the jar, the memory
book and its pictures, paintings, room pictures, letters, tricks, decorations, school words,
clothes, the garden and more. Make one every few weeks, and before moving to
a new tablet.

**To make a backup:** open the grown-up corner (hold the top-left corner for
3 seconds), tap **Backup**, then **Save a backup**.
- **iPad:** a share menu opens. Tap **Save to Files**, choose a folder (for
  example *On My iPad* or *iCloud Drive*), and tap **Save**.
- **Surface:** the file goes to the **Downloads** folder. You may want to copy
  it somewhere safe, like OneDrive.

The file is called something like `twin-pets-backup-2026-10-02.json`.

**To restore from a backup:** grown-up corner → **Backup** → **Restore from a
backup**, then pick the file. The game shows what's in it (how many hearts
and memory book days) and asks before replacing anything. If you change your
mind afterwards, the same page has **Undo the last restore**.

## If a new version breaks something: undo it on GitHub

Every change to the game arrives as a pull request that you merge. If a new
version has a problem, you can undo it in a few taps, and the website goes
back to the version before:

1. On GitHub, open the repository and click **Pull requests**.
2. Click **Closed** and open the pull request you merged most recently.
3. Near the bottom of the page, click **Revert**. GitHub makes a new pull
   request that undoes it.
4. On that new pull request, click **Merge pull request**, then
   **Confirm merge**.
5. Wait a few minutes, then close the game on the tablet completely and open
   it again.

The girls' progress is saved on the tablet, not on GitHub, so undoing a
version doesn't erase anything they did. To be extra safe, make a backup
before trying a new version. Later, when the problem is fixed, the change can
be merged again.

## Changing things yourself

Open `js/settings.js` in any text editor, or on GitHub by clicking the file
and then the ✏️ pencil. The section called **1. SETTINGS — safe to change**
lets you change:

- the girls' and pets' names
- how many together moments it takes for the first new things to appear
- how many hearts fill the jar
- which words the pets ask for in thought bubbles
- each pet's favorite food
- how fast tummies empty, how long brushed fur stays fluffy, how many tries it
  takes to learn a trick, and how long the garden takes to grow
- how many new things appear each time the game is opened
- how many seconds the girls have to tap the same room on the map

**Your own pet pictures:** see [`art/README.md`](art/README.md).

## Tests

The game has automatic tests for the important things: saving and loading
(including saves from every earlier version), backups and restoring, moving
between rooms, the friendship jar, the grown-up corner, music and sound
switches, and the start screen.

- **On GitHub:** they run by themselves on every pull request. A green ✓ next
  to the pull request means they all passed; a red ✗ means something needs a
  look before merging.
- **On any browser:** open the game's address with `tests/` at the end (for
  example `https://zeusbrucesantana.github.io/twin-pets/tests/`) and tap
  **Run the tests**. They take about a minute. They use their own pretend save,
  so the girls' real progress is never touched.
